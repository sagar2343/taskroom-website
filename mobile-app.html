The Mobile App

One app. Two roles.
Total accountability.

Managers control everything from the web dashboard. Employees work from their phones. TaskRoom is where the actual field work gets done — and proved.

The TaskRoom Android app is a GPS field employee tracking app for delivery, sales, and service teams in India. It logs geofenced attendance, captures photo proof and digital signatures per task, and works fully offline until connectivity returns.
