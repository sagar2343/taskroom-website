Multi-Step Task Workflows

Break any job into ordered steps. Each step has a deadline, location requirement, and proof requirements. Employees can't skip steps or mark things done without completing each gate.

Photo Proof of Work

Require employees to upload a photo before a step can be marked complete. The app forces a live camera capture — no uploading old photos. Each photo is timestamped and stored permanently.

  • ✓Live camera capture required

  • ✓Viewable instantly in dashboard

Customers draw their signature directly on the employee's phone screen. Captured as an image, tied to the task step, and stored with a timestamp. Eliminates paper POD forms entirely.
